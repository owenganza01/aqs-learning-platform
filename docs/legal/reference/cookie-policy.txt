Cookie Policy
This policy explains how AQS Learning, the e-learning platform of AQS (“we”,
“us” or “our”), uses cookies and similar technologies (collectively, “cookies”).
It applies to all authorized internal and external users of the platform, and it
also explains your choices regarding cookies.

Please read this policy together with the AQS E-Learning Privacy Policy, which
sets out how we handle personal data and how to exercise your rights.

What are cookies?
Cookies are small text files placed on your device by your web browser when
you use AQS Learning. They help the platform recognize your device and
remember information about your visit. Session cookies last only for your visit
and are deleted when you close your browser. Persistent cookies remain on
your device for a set period or until you delete them. Cookies set by AQS
Learning itself are first-party cookies; those set by another organization are
third-party cookies. Similar technologies, such as local storage and session
storage, work in a comparable way.

How do we use cookies?
AQS Learning uses cookies only for the purposes below. To the extent that the
data collected constitutes personal data, we process it in accordance with
the AQS E-Learning Privacy Policy.

Essential cookies. Necessary for the basic operation of AQS Learning,
including to keep you signed in as you move between pages, to sign you out
securely, and to protect your account and the platform against unauthorized
access and misuse. These cookies are automatically enabled and cannot be
turned off because the platform cannot function without them. They do not
require your consent.

AQS Learning does not use functional, analytics, third-party or advertising
and marketing cookies, and does not use cookies to build user profiles or to
track you across other websites. If we introduce any of these, we will update
this policy first and will not set any cookie that requires consent until you have
actively given it. You will then be able to accept or refuse each category
and change your choice at any time through a cookie preference control on
the platform.
What are your choices regarding cookies?
Because AQS Learning currently uses only essential cookies, there are no
optional cookie settings to manage on the platform. You can delete cookies
or instruct your web browser to block them through your browser’s settings.

Please note that if you delete or block essential cookies, you might not be
able to sign in, the platform might not work correctly, and your session may
end unexpectedly. Deleting cookies does not delete the learning records we
hold about you.
