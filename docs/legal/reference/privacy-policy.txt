1. Key Information
Africa Quantitative Sciences ("AQS") is the data controller for the personal
data processed through AQS Learning, meaning that AQS decides why and
how that data is processed. AQS Learning is the e-learning platform through
which AQS provides courses and lessons to its personnel and authorized
external users.
AQS collects the personal data described in Section 4, including account
and identity details, course and learning records, assessment results,
messages, and technical and usage information. AQS uses it for the purposes
set out in Section 5, which include managing accounts, delivering courses,
recording progress and completion, evidencing required training, supporting
communication about learning and keeping the platform secure.
You have rights in relation to your personal data, as set out in Section 11. You

2. Purpose and Who We Are
This Privacy Policy explains how AQS collects, uses, stores, shares and
otherwise processes personal data through AQS Learning. AQS processes
personal data in line with Law No. 058/2021 of 13/10/2021 relating to the
protection of personal data and privacy (the "Law"), which is supervised by
the Data Protection and Privacy Office of the National Cyber Security
Authority (NCSA).
Please read this Privacy Policy carefully to understand what AQS does with
your personal data. If you do not understand any part of it, or if you have a
question about how your personal data is handled, please contact us using
the details below.
Legal entity: Africa Quantitative Sciences
Data Protection Officer: Janice Dorcas Mukeshimana
Privacy contact: info@africaqs.com
In this Policy, "personal data" means any information relating to an identified
or identifiable person, and "processing" means any operation performed on
personal data, including collecting, storing, using, sharing and deleting it.
"Internal users" are AQS employees and other AQS personnel, and "external
users" are persons outside AQS who have been authorized to access AQS
Learning.

3. What Information This Privacy Policy Covers
This Policy applies to the personal data processed in connection with the use
of AQS Learning by everyone whose personal data is processed through it,
including:
    • AQS employees and other internal users who hold a learner account;
    • authorized external users who have been given access to AQS
        Learning; and
    • trainers, course owners, course administrators and platform
        administrators, whether internal or external, whose account and
        activity data is also processed.
You are responsible for ensuring that the personal data you provide is
accurate and kept up to date, because inaccurate information may affect
your access to courses and AQS's ability to contact you. This Policy should be
read together with AQS's information security policies and acceptable-use
rules, the AQS Learning Cookie Policy, AQS's employment terms (for internal
users) and any terms under which an external user is given access.

4. What Personal Data We Collect
AQS collects only the personal data needed to run the platform. We may
collect and process your personal data:
   • directly from you, for example when you provide account information,
     take an assessment or communicate with us;
   • as you use AQS Learning, through progress records, activity logs and
     the device you use to access the platform; and
   • from AQS records and other users, for example when your account is
     created or a trainer records feedback.

4.1 Personal Data we collect directly from you
   • Account and identity data: your name, email address or username,
     login credentials and, where held, your job title and department.
   • Assessment data, where assessments are used: your answers and
     attempts.
   • Messages: the content of messages you send through the platform,
     with recipient and time.
   • Support and administrative data: requests to administrators, profile or
     account changes and records of privacy requests you make.

4.2 Personal Data we collect as you use AQS Learning and from your device
    • Course and learning data: the courses you are enrolled in or have
       selected, lessons started and completed, completion percentage, last
       course and lesson accessed, and dates of completion.
    • Assessment results, where assessments are used: scores, results,
       feedback and any certificates issued.
    • Technical and usage data: such as your IP address, browser and
       device type, login times, and security and audit logs, together with
       your user role and access permissions.
Cookies and similar technologies. AQS Learning uses cookies and similar
technologies to operate the platform, keep you signed in and protect your
account. The cookies used, their purposes and their duration are set out in
the AQS Learning Cookie Policy, which should be read together with this
Policy. Where a cookie is not strictly necessary for the platform to work, AQS
will ask for your consent before it is set.

4.3 Personal Data we collect from AQS records and other users
   • AQS records and access arrangements: where your account is created
     or updated using information AQS already holds or receives when your
     access is arranged, such as your name, email address, department or
     user role.
   • Other users: such as a trainer who records feedback about your
     learning, or another user who sends you a message.

4.4 Sensitive Personal Data
AQS Learning is not designed to collect sensitive personal data, such as
health information, religious or political beliefs, ethnic origin or biometric data.
Please do not enter such information in messages or free-text fields. The
platform is intended for adult users.

6. Learning and Assessment Records
Your learning record is the core record created by AQS Learning. It shows the
courses you are enrolled in, your progress, the lessons you have completed
and when, and, where assessments are used, your results. AQS uses it to:
   • let you resume learning where you stopped and view your own
     progress;
    • allow authorized trainers, administrators and managers to confirm that
       required training has been completed;
    • produce participation and completion reports, using aggregated
       figures wherever individual detail is not needed; and
    • provide evidence of training for internal and external audits.
Who may see these records is set out in Section 7.1, and how long they are
kept is set out in Section 8. AQS does not make decisions about you based
solely on automated processing of your learning data where those decisions
have legal or similarly significant effects. If you believe a record is inaccurate,
for example a lesson wrongly shown as incomplete, you may ask for it to be
corrected (see Section 11).

7. How We Share Personal Data
AQS does not sell personal data and does not share it with third parties for
their own marketing. This section distinguishes between access to personal
data within AQS Learning by authorized users performing their roles, and
disclosure of personal data to third parties outside AQS.

7.1 Access within AQS Learning
Access to personal data within AQS Learning is limited to authorized users
who need it for their organizational responsibilities, whether they are internal
or external users, and each role sees only the minimum data required for its
function.
   • You can see your own profile, courses, progress, messages and results.
   • Trainers and course owners, whether internal or external, can see the
       progress and results of learners on their own courses, and messages
       exchanged with those learners.
   • Line managers and HR or training coordinators can see training
       completion information about the users they are responsible for, as
       needed to administer and confirm required training.
   • Platform administrators and IT staff can access account and technical
       data to support users and maintain the platform, and access personal
       data in databases only where necessary for support, maintenance or
       incident response.
   • The Data Protection Officer can access data needed to handle privacy
       requests, investigations, audits and breach response.
   • Management receives aggregated reports, and individual records only
       where a specific, documented need exists.
Messages are intended for their recipients. Administrators do not routinely
read them, and access to message content is limited to situations such as a
security incident, a suspected breach of AQS policy or a legal requirement,
and requires authorization. Everyone with access to personal data through
AQS Learning is bound by confidentiality obligations. Access by authorized
users in these roles is not disclosure to third parties.
7.2 Disclosure to third parties
Personal data is disclosed to third parties outside AQS only in these
circumstances:
   • Service providers, such as hosting, technical support or notification
     providers, process data on AQS's behalf. Any provider acting as a data
     processor must be bound by a written agreement requiring it to act
     only on AQS's instructions, apply appropriate security measures, keep
     the data confidential and notify AQS of any personal data breach
     within forty-eight (48) hours of becoming aware of it. Providers are not
     permitted to use the data for their own purposes.
   • Auditors and certification bodies, where needed to verify training,
     limited to aggregated information wherever individual detail is not
     required.
   • Regulators, courts and law enforcement, only where required by law or
     valid legal process, and limited to what is required.

8. Retention of Personal Data
AQS keeps personal data only for as long as needed for the purposes for
which it was collected, or for longer where the Law permits, such as where
retention is required by law or contract, or is needed to establish or defend
legal claims. In practice:
   • account data is kept while your account is active and is deactivated
       when your access ends, for example when an internal user leaves AQS
       or an external user's access is withdrawn;
   • learning, completion and assessment records are kept to evidence
       training and meet audit requirements, for the period stated below; and
   • messages, technical and audit logs, privacy request records and
       backups are kept only for as long as necessary for their purpose.
Specific retention period: AQS retains learning, completion and assessment
records for five (5) years after completion. All other personal data is retained
only for as long as necessary to fulfil the purpose for which it was collected,
unless a longer period is required or permitted by law. Once personal data is
no longer required, AQS securely deletes or anonymizes it so that it can no
longer identify you.

9. Confidentiality and Security of Personal Data
AQS takes appropriate and reasonable technical and organizational
measures to protect personal data against loss, damage, destruction and
unauthorized access, as the Law requires. These measures include role-based
access limits, secure authentication and protection of data in transit and
storage, together with logging and monitoring, backup and recovery
arrangements, security updates and confidentiality obligations for staff. AQS
identifies foreseeable risks, tests its safeguards and updates them as risks
change, and requires service providers to meet equivalent standards.
No system is completely secure, so AQS also relies on users to follow the
responsibilities in Section 14. Please also note that email communications are
not necessarily secure, and personal data should not be sent by email unless
necessary.

10. Personal Data Breaches
A personal data breach is a security incident that leads to accidental or
unlawful destruction, loss, alteration, unauthorized disclosure of, or access to,
personal data. Anyone who suspects a breach involving AQS Learning must
report it immediately to the Data Protection Officer or the IT service desk.
Where a breach occurs, AQS will:
   • notify the NCSA Data Protection and Privacy Office within forty-eight
     (48) hours of becoming aware of it, and submit a report with all
     available facts within seventy-two (72) hours;
   • inform affected users without undue delay where the breach is likely to
     have serious consequences for their rights, explaining what happened,
     the likely effects, the measures taken and whom to contact; and
   • document each breach, its effects and the action taken.

11. Your Rights
Under the Law, you have the right to:
   •   access your personal data and information about how it is processed;
   •   rectification of inaccurate or incomplete data;
   •   erasure of your data in the circumstances the Law provides;
   •   restriction of how your data is used, in certain circumstances;
   •   object to processing where there are no overriding legitimate grounds
       for it;
   • portability, to receive your data in a structured, commonly used
       format, where the Law applies;
   • not be subject to solely automated decisions with legal or similarly
       significant effects;
   • withdraw consent at any time, where processing is based on consent;
       and
   • designate an heir to your data rights and be represented by another
       person with written authorization.
To exercise a right, write to the privacy contact in Section 2, stating the right
you wish to exercise and the data concerned. AQS may need to confirm
your identity first. Requests are free of charge. AQS will respond in writing or
electronically within thirty (30) days and will explain any refusal. Some rights
may be limited where the Law allows AQS to keep data, for example training
records that AQS must retain as evidence of required training.
If AQS agrees to a request for erasure, it will delete or anonymize the personal
data concerned in AQS Learning, subject to any retention that is required by
law or needed to evidence required training, as described in Section 8.
Where AQS keeps certain data despite your request, it will explain why in its
response.

12. Questions, Suggestions and Complaints
If you have a question about this Policy or a concern about how AQS
handles your personal data, contact the Data Protection Officer using the
details in Section 2. AQS will try to resolve complaints internally first.
If you are not satisfied with AQS's response, you may appeal to the Data
Protection and Privacy Office of the National Cyber Security Authority (NCSA)
within thirty (30) days of receiving it, and the Office will respond within sixty
(60) days of receiving your appeal. You may also lodge a complaint with the
Office directly (website: dpo.gov.rw; email: dpp@dpo.gov.rw) or bring a case
before the competent court.

13. Data Storage and International Transfers
The Law requires personal data to be stored in Rwanda unless the controller
or processor holds a registration certificate authorizing storage or transfer
outside Rwanda. AQS will transfer personal data outside Rwanda only where
the Law permits, for example with NCSA authorization following proof of
appropriate safeguards, with your consent, or where the Law otherwise
allows.
AQS Learning data is hosted in Rwanda and is not stored or processed
outside Rwanda.

14. User Responsibilities
To help protect personal data, all users must:
   • keep login credentials confidential and not share their account;
   • not enter sensitive personal data, or personal data about other people,
     in messages or free-text fields unless necessary for a training purpose;
   • not access another user's data, or copy or export learner data, except
     as their role permits;
   • sign out on shared devices;
   • report suspected breaches, lost devices or suspicious activity
     immediately; and
   • keep their profile information accurate and up to date.
